BOYO Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

BOYO is a card game. We collect as little information as possible to run online games.

What we process

  • Nickname — the name you type, shown to other players in your room.
  • Game state — cards, moves and scores of the rooms you play in.
  • Room credential — a random token stored on your device so you can reconnect to your seat. The server keeps only a one-way hash of it.

We do not ask for accounts, email addresses, contacts, location, photos or payment details. BOYO does not include analytics or advertising trackers.

Storage and retention

Rooms are hosted on Cloudflare Workers / Durable Objects. A room and everything in it is deleted automatically after 6 hours without activity. Your nickname, sound setting and room tokens stay in your device's local storage until you clear it.

Platforms

When you play on CrazyGames, the CrazyGames SDK is loaded and CrazyGames' own privacy policy also applies.

Children

BOYO does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Nicknames are free text; do not use your real full name.
